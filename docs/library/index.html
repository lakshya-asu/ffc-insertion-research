What comes next

Resolve mounted jaw contact, verify loaded FR3 lift and stopping, then add held-end camera inspection. Sensor-guided alignment is the first planned learned motion skill. Imitation learning precedes contact RL; frozen evaluation scenes remain separate from training.

Native FR3 replay export, complete rerun bundles, calibrated camera episodes and parallel-training resource profiling are still pending. Failed mounted runs remain in the local experiment archive.
